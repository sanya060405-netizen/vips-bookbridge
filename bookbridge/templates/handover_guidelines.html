VIPS Campus Safety Protocol

Approved Campus Handover Guidelines

To safeguard student privacy and ensure physical safety, Vivekananda Institute of Professional Studies strictly enforces book handovers exclusively within approved on-campus locations. Personal residential addresses and off-campus meetups are prohibited.

Daylight Hours Only

All handovers must occur between 8:30 AM and 6:00 PM on active academic working days when faculty and security are present.

Designated Campus Spots

Meet strictly at designated high-traffic points like the Central Library, SAC Atrium, or Department Lab lobbies. Never off-campus.

Inspect on the Spot

Verify edition, missing pages, and highlighted notes in person before confirming receipt on BookBridge.

Zero Cash Exchange

BookBridge is built for Lending, Book-for-Book Exchange, and Free Donation. Commercial selling is prohibited.

Step-by-Step Flow

How Handover Works on BookBridge

A clear 4-step lifecycle ensures both students agree on the book condition, borrowing timeframe, and handover location before physically meeting.

1
Student Submits Interest

Click 'Express Interest', state why you need the book, and select an approved campus spot.

2
Owner Accepts & Gives Time

Book owner reviews in their dashboard, accepts, and writes a meeting note (e.g. "Tomorrow 1:30 PM outside Library").

3
Campus Handover & Inspection

Both students meet at the designated spot, inspect book pages/edition, and hand over the material.

4
Confirm Completion

Owner clicks 'Confirm Book Handed Over'. Book status updates to 'Completed', keeping the catalogue clean.
